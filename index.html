📲 Setup Guide: Add to Home Screen & TalkBack Tips ▼

1. How to Add to Home Screen (Android & iPhone)

  • Android (Chrome): Tap the three-dot menu (top right) and select "Add to Home screen" or "Install app". It will place a full-screen icon on your friend's phone home screen.
  • iPhone (Safari): Tap the Share button at the bottom and select "Add to Home Screen".

2. How Your Blind Friend Uses It with TalkBack

  • Center of Screen: Place a finger right in the middle of the screen to hear "Play Video" or "Pause Video", then double-tap anywhere on the screen to toggle Play/Pause.
  • Left & Right of Center: Slide finger slightly left for Previous Video or slightly right for Next Video, then double-tap.
  • Load Phone Videos: Tap "Load Phone Videos" at the top right to select multiple MP4 or audio videos stored on the phone.
